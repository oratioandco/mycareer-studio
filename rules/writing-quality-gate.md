# Writing Quality Gate

Applied to: All CV outputs, cover letters, profile texts, and any client-facing writing.

## Purpose
Every piece of writing must pass as human-authored. No AI slop, no jargon, no keyword stuffing. The reader is a human hiring manager or recruiter — write for them, not for an ATS bot.

## Rules

### 1. No AI Slop
- ❌ No compound sentences chained with em-dashes, semicolons, and "— including..." 
- ❌ No lists disguised as sentences ("X, Y, and Z — with A, B, and C")
- ❌ No filler intensifiers ("deep experience," "extensive track record," "proven ability")
- ❌ No buzzword stacking ("AI-native design practice," "agentic prototyping")
- ❌ No sentence over 25 words in a profile paragraph
- ✅ Short, direct sentences with natural rhythm
- ✅ Concrete > abstract ("designed filter logic" > "leveraged synergies")
- ✅ Say one thing per sentence

### 2. No American Startup Bro Language
- ❌ "hunger," "grit," "passion," "scrappy"
- ❌ "disrupted," "revolutionized," "10x'd"
- ❌ "moved the needle," "level up," "crushed it"
- ❌ "driving," "owning," "leaning in"
- ✅ "initiative," "curiosity," "built," "established," "led"
- ✅ German/European professional tone: competent, specific, understated

### 3. No Jargon Without Context
- ❌ "AI content pipeline," "RAG orchestration," "design-system-as-API"
- ✅ "AI-assisted translation tools," "design system synced across platforms"
- Technical terms are fine when the audience expects them; strip them when writing for general/hybrid audiences

### 4. Human Voice Test
Read every paragraph out loud. If it sounds like a LinkedIn influencer wrote it, rewrite it. If a hiring manager would roll their eyes, rewrite it. If it sounds like something you'd actually say in an interview, keep it.

### 5. Density Check
- Profile paragraph: max 3 sentences, max 60 words
- Bullet points: max 25 words each
- One idea per bullet, not three chained with semicolons
- If a bullet needs a semicolon, it's probably two bullets

## Checklist (run before finalizing any output)
- [ ] Read every sentence out loud — does it sound human?
- [ ] No sentence over 25 words in profile
- [ ] No bullet over 25 words
- [ ] No semicolons in bullets
- [ ] No em-dash chains (max one em-dash per paragraph)
- [ ] No buzzwords from the banned list
- [ ] Concrete and specific, not vague and impressive
- [ ] Would a German hiring manager understand every word?
- [ ] ATS keywords are in the hidden layer and bullet points, not the profile

## Source
Established 2026-04-24 after LiveEO CV review identified AI-generated tone, keyword stuffing in profile, jargon in project descriptions, and American startup language inappropriate for German/European audience.
